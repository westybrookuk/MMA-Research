- Anything below is a **risk screen**, not an availability guarantee or legal opinion. Final word: a trademark professional / attorney search in the relevant classes (likely 35, 39, 41, 42 and possibly 43/45) in UK, EU, US and Thailand.

## 2. Headline findings

1. **`abroadmate.com` is NOT free** — it is a parked premium domain for sale via **HugeDomains at US$3,595** (or 24 × $149.79). It is not an active product, so there is no live competitor at that exact domain — but the `.com` is unavailable at normal cost, and buying it would **not** by itself clear any trademark risk.
2. **`abroadmates.com` (plural) is an ACTIVE startup** — **"AbroadMates" by The Abroad Company**: an AI-powered 1:1 **study-abroad mentorship marketplace** ("Talk to your AbroadMate", paid mentor calls) that already uses a **country-edition pattern including `ThailandMates` / study-in-thailand**, and whose founder publicly states it is **expanding into visa assistance and relocation support**. It has a live site, YouTube `@abroadmates`, Facebook and LinkedIn. This is the most serious conflict: near-identical name (singular/plural), overlapping "moving/living abroad" audience, and explicit expansion into **our exact space (relocation/visa)**.
3. **`thailandmate.com` is an ACTIVE business** — a Korean-operated **Thailand local-booking directory** ("ThailandMate — find & book restaurants, massage, stays, golf, taxis"; 7 languages, ~804 businesses, Pattaya/Bangkok focus), hosted and indexed with verification records. A separate older **`thailandmate.net`** travel site ("Thailand Mate" — hotels, visa, insurance content; ~15k Facebook) also exists. So **"ThailandMate" is already a live Thailand travel brand — twice.**
4. The **"-Mate" relocation/travel naming field is crowded**: Migrate Mate (US immigration/visa jobs, active socials), Nomadbase/​NomadBase (active digital-nomad community + apps), MoverMate, MoveMate, TravelMate TM. This weakens the distinctiveness of any "…Mate" name and increases confusion/likelihood-of-dilution arguments in adjacent classes.
5. Free TLDs exist for `abroadmate` (`.io`, `.app`, `.co.uk` return no DNS records) and `thailandmate` (`.io`, `.co.uk`), but **a free ccTLD/gTLD does not resolve the brand conflicts** above — the live businesses and the crowded "-mate" space remain the issue, not the domain extension.

## 3. Recommendations (verdict)

### 3.1 Is `AbroadMate` safe enough as the global working brand?
**Not as it stands — treat as a clear conflict to move away from before public launch.** Reasoning (risk, not legal ruling):
- A near-identical active brand (AbroadMates / The Abroad Company) already serves cross-border movers and is publicly expanding into **visa assistance and relocation** — the same sector and a singular/plural name a user would confuse. They operate in education→relocation, have live social handles (`@abroadmates`), and use a "country + Mate" edition scheme that collides directly with "ThailandMate."
- The exact `.com` is a premium listing ($3,595), so the natural domain is also unavailable at normal cost.
- Even though a formal TM register search is still required, the *commercial* conflict alone is enough to avoid investing in branding, domains or handles under this name.
- **[REC]** Do not register domains/socials or print "AbroadMate" publicly. Keep it as an internal codename only until a replacement is chosen; if there is strong attachment, have a professional run UK IPO + EUIPO + WIPO + USPTO and assess the singular/plural and class overlap — but expectation should be to rebrand.

### 3.2 Does `ThailandMate` work as the destination edition?
**No — retire it.** A live `thailandmate.com` travel-booking directory in the very destination, plus `thailandmate.net`, means the edition name is already used in-sector in Thailand; it also inherits the global "AbroadMate/AbroadMates" conflict and matches the study-abroad company's own `ThailandMates` page. A Thai first-to-file trademark regime (see §5) adds risk for a Thailand-facing brand. The destination edition should follow whatever new global root is chosen (see §4 and `abroadmate-alternative-names.md`).

### 3.3 Five alternative global names
Short, distinct, available on primary TLDs **per this DNS screen** (recheck + professional TM search before buying):
1. **RelocBase** — relocbase .com/.io/.app/.co/.co.uk all returned no DNS records; descriptive, fits a "base/toolkit" platform with destination editions.
2. **Relocadi** — coined/fanciful word; .com/.io/.app/.co/.net/.co.uk all clear on DNS; stronger distinctiveness (verify meaning/pronunciation in Thai and other languages).
3. **NewInDesk** — "new in [city]" desk/guide idea; .com/.io/.app/.co.uk clear on DNS; friendly, edition-friendly.
4. **SettleGuide / SettleDesk** — "settle" framing (note: `settleabroad.com` and several parked "…mate/…base" .coms are taken; confirm the exact Settle* domain before choosing).
5. **FirstStretch / FirstNinety** — leans on the signature "First 90 Days" product identity (note: `first90.com` is taken; a coined variant like **FirstStretch** had inconclusive DNS — recheck).

Full rationale, the edition names each implies, and the ones explicitly rejected are in **`abroadmate-alternative-names.md`**.

### 3.4 Suggested naming hierarchy
- **Global parent brand / platform:** one distinctive root (e.g. **RelocBase** or **Relocadi**) — the app, company and umbrella site.
- **Destination editions:** **`[City/Country] + [Root]`** or **`[Root] [Place]`**, consistently, e.g. *Bangkok RelocBase* / *relocbase.com/bangkok*; later *Chiang Mai*, *Lisbon*, *Mexico City*. Avoid a separate made-up word per city (it multiplies clearances) — use subpaths or a predictable prefix.
- **Products:** descriptive and sub-branded under the root — e.g. "RelocBase First 90 Days: Bangkok".
- **Tagline/descriptor always adjacent to the logo** at launch ("independent, sourced relocation guides") until the mark is established and, ideally, registered.

## 4. Trademark registers to search (professionally)
- **UK IPO** — https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark (UK marks; word + class).
- **EUIPO** — https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/ (EU trademarks; TMview also pulls UK/national).
- **WIPO Global Brand Database** — https://branddb.wipo.int/branddb/en/ (Madrid/international registrations designating UK/EU/US/TH).
- **Thailand DIP** — https://search.ipthailand.go.th/ (live; ~1.31m trademark records) and Nice-class keyword tool https://tmsearch.ipthailand.go.th/. Thailand is **first-to-file** and class-specific; search both Latin and Thai-script/phonetic equivalents. In Feb 2026 the DIP added free "AI Image Search" and "Trademark Checker" tools (preliminary screening only, per legal commentary — they do not replace a clearance search).
- Recommended classes for a content/tools/possible-marketplace relocation brand: **35** (information/directory/affiliate commerce), **39** (travel/transport info), **41** (guides/education/content), **42** (SaaS/software/app), and consider **43** (accommodation referrals) / **45** (legal/visa *information* referrals) depending on scope. A professional should finalise classes.
