# AbroadMate / ThailandMate — Partner Outreach Templates

**Status:** Cold/introductory outreach drafts only. **Sending these does not create a partnership, and nothing in them states or implies that any provider is approved, vetted, suitable, or affiliated with AbroadMate/ThailandMate.** No commission rates are quoted, no approval is claimed, and no regulated advice is offered.
**Created:** 28 August 2026. Use: fill the `[bracketed]` fields, send from the role partnerships address, log replies in the partner tracker, and route any regulated (insurance/visa/legal) responses to the ⚖ reviewer before listing.

> **Standing position to state in every thread:** ThailandMate is an independent, source-dated research guide for people moving to Bangkok. We list tools and official sources for readers' own research; we are not an agent, broker, insurer or adviser, and we do not make regulated recommendations. Any future link would be clearly disclosed as a partner/affiliate link.

Every template asks the same eight things: **(1)** partner-program availability; **(2)** Thailand availability; **(3)** affiliate vs lead-generation structure; **(4)** disclosure requirements they impose; **(5)** tracking requirements (cookies/pixels); **(6)** data-sharing obligations; **(7)** licence/regulatory information; **(8)** refund and customer-support responsibility.

---

## How to use (brief)
- Send one variant per provider; keep it short and factual.
- Do **not** promise placement, traffic, reviews or "our readers will…" claims.
- If a programme is self-serve (affiliate network), note the link and still complete the partner tracker; do not sign agreements until the ⚖/business review.
- For insurance and visa/legal providers: only ever link to the provider or to official sources; no product recommendations.

## Response-handling rules (all sectors)
1. Log every reply in the private partner tracker: provider, category, Thailand availability, programme type, tracking/data needs, licence status, refund/support owner, date.
2. **Do not sign** any agreement, accept terms or place a tracking pixel until the privacy/legal (and, where relevant, tax) review is done.
3. No provider is described as "vetted," "approved," "recommended" or a "partner" until an agreement actually exists and is disclosed; until then they are "research candidates."
4. Any provider-set data sharing beyond an outbound link triggers a DPA/joint-controllership review ⚖.
5. Regulated sectors (insurance, visa/legal, accommodation/agency) additionally require business/tax review 🌍 before any link is published.
