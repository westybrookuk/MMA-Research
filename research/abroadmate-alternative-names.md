## 2. Rejected / not recommended (with reasons from the screen)

- **Anything ending in "-Mate"** (e.g. ExpatMate, MoveMate, LandMate, NomadMate): crowded — Migrate Mate (visa/relocation), MoverMate, MoveMate, TravelMate, and the direct AbroadMates conflict. High confusion risk, weak marks.
- **"…Base" nomad names** incl. **NomadBase / AwayBase / ThaiBase**: Nomadbase is an active nomad community + apps; awaybase.com and thaibase.com are registered/parked. Avoid the base-with-nomad pairing specifically.
- **LandingPad, Arriba/ArribaGo, NewHome, LandGuide, PortGuide, PackMate, First90, AbroadKit, ThailandKit, BangkokKit, BangkokNest, ThaiNest(.com), SettleAbroad(.com)**: generic phrase or primary TLD registered/parked; weak distinctiveness or taken. (ThailandKit .io/.app/.co are free but the .com is held by a hostinglotus Thai registrant — avoid.)
- **ThailandMate / ThaiMate**: live in-sector businesses (thailandmate.com, thailandmate.net) — do not use.

## 3. Suggested naming hierarchy

```
GLOBAL PARENT BRAND (company + platform + app)
   e.g.  RelocBase  (or Relocadi)
   - always shown with a descriptor at launch:
     "Independent, sourced relocation guides"

DESTINATION EDITIONS (predictable pattern, not new words)
   [City] [Root]   or   [Root] · [City]
   e.g.  Bangkok RelocBase   /   RelocBase Bangkok
         relocbase.com/bangkok
   later: Chiang Mai · Lisbon · Mexico City · Ho Chi Minh …

PRODUCTS (descriptive, under the parent)
   e.g.  "RelocBase First 90 Days: Bangkok"
         "RelocBase First 7 Days" (free checklist)
         "RelocBase Budget Planner"

FREE vs PAID (naming stays independent of brand):
   free = checklists, matcher, methodology
   paid = the "First 90 Days" edition pack
```

Principles:
1. **One root, many places** — edition = place + root, so each new city does not need a fresh trademark/domain set.
2. **Own the root, not the descriptor** — keep "First 90 Days", "checklist", "matcher" as descriptive product terms (harder to own) and put the trademark weight on the root.
3. **Domain strategy** — secure the root `.com` (primary), plus `.io/.app/.co.uk` defensively; editions live as subpaths (e.g. `/bangkok`) rather than separate names where possible.
4. **Handles** — same spelling across YouTube/X/Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn/Facebook; reserve the root handle, use `[root]bangkok` only if the bare handle is unavailable; verify manually logged out.
5. **Tagline until established** — show the descriptor beside the logo until marks are filed; Thailand is first-to-file, so file early there if Thailand is a priority market (via a Thai trademark professional).
