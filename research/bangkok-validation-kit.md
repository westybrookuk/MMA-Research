# ThailandMate — Bangkok Validation Kit

**Date:** 28 August 2026
**Hypothesis under test [REC]:** *Self-funded/DIY relocating families moving to Bangkok will pay for a structured "First-90-Days" planner that de-risks school, housing-near-school, budget, visas and healthcare — and a subset will want vetted referrals (property, school-admission advisors, family-insurance brokers).* Nomads are the secondary/affiliate layer.

**Tags:** [ASSUMPTION] all thresholds and prices are hypotheses · [LEGAL-RISK] items need official-source framing. No traffic figures, commission rates, or competitor prices are invented. Stack: one-page site + email capture + presale checkout + booking link. 30 days, low cost.

---

## 1. Twelve customer interview questions

Target **12 interviews** (~7 families, ~5 nomads), 25 min each. Mix people *pre-arrival* and *within first 6 months*. Do not pitch during the interview; capture behaviour and spending.

**Families (primary):**
1. Walk me through your move to Bangkok — where are you in the process (deciding, school applications, arrived, settled)?
2. How did you choose your kids' school? What did it cost in total, and what surprised you about fees or the application?
3. How did you decide where to live relative to the school? What did the commute turn out to be?
4. What broke or stressed you most in the first 90 days — housing, visa, banking, healthcare, transport, help at home?
5. How did visas work for your spouse and kids, and what was confusing? *([LEGAL-RISK] — listen; don't advise.)*
6. How did you arrange family health insurance and healthcare for the children? Any surprises?
7. Where did you get trusted advice (school networks, Facebook, agents, blogs), and what did you **pay** for?
8. If a single planner had walked you through school + area + budget + visa + healthcare before arrival, would you have bought it? What would make it worth paying for — and what would you expect to pay? *(Let them name a price.)*

**Nomads (secondary):**
9. What brought you to Bangkok rather than Chiang Mai or the islands?
10. How did banking go on your visa, and what did you do about the 180-day tax threshold?
11. What did you pay for in your first 90 days (insurance, eSIM, coliving, agent, coworking)?
12. What's one question you could never find a reliable answer to — and would you pay for a planner or prefer a referral to a vetted person?

**Interpretation:** score each for (a) an expensive, time-boxed, recently-hit mistake, (b) evidence of prior spending, (c) which deliverable they'd pay for, (d) family vs nomad vs overlap (remote-working parents).

## 5. Suggested success criteria (30 days)

**All figures are starting hypotheses [ASSUMPTION] — set before launch; use as go/no-go.**

| Metric | Target (30 days) | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| Landing-page visitors | **1,000+** | Distribution reach (families are harder to reach than nomads — weight toward parent groups/SEO) |
| Email sign-ups (free calculator/lead magnet) | **250–400+** | Resonance; family lead magnets (school-fee + commute tools) vs nomad tools |
| Customer interviews | **12** (7 families / 5 nomads) | Qualitative depth |
| **Planner pre-orders** | **12–20+** (family offer) | Willingness to pay; note which tier (self-serve vs +expert vs +referrals) |
| Pre-order conversion (of email list) | **3–6%+** | Offer strength; compare family vs nomad price points |
| **Affiliate / partner clicks** | **150+** tracked (insurance/eSIM/VPN + property/school/broker leads) | Monetisation intent and which category leads |
| Vetted-referral / lead enquiries | **5–10+** | High-value lead-gen (property, school-admission, family insurance) |

**Go / no-go logic [REC]:**
- **Green (build family planner):** ≥250 emails with the **family** lead magnets outperforming, **and** ≥12 family-leaning pre-orders, **and** 5+ lead enquiries → build the Bangkok Family First-90-Days Planner and partner pipeline.
- **Amber (nomads convert, families don't yet):** strong nomad signups/affiliate clicks but few family pre-orders → ship nomad tools/pack for cash flow, keep building family SEO/lead-gen and re-test.
- **Red (no family traction):** weak family sign-ups and no pre-orders → question the Bangkok-family assumption (maybe DIY families are too few vs corporate-package) before investing further.

**Guardrails:** no personalised tax/visa/insurance advice ([LEGAL-RISK]); link official sources (thaievisa.go.th, immigration.go.th, rd.go.th, oic.or.th, isat.or.th, BTS/MRT); never promise approvals or account openings; show a last-updated date; do not state commission rates or unverified competitor pricing; refund presales if the planner doesn't ship on schedule.
