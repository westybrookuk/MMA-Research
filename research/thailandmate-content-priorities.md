# ThailandMate — Content & SEO Priorities

**Research date:** 28 August 2026 · **Companion to:** `thailandmate-opportunity-report.md` and `thailandmate-source-map.csv`
**Posture:** nomad-first (DTV), Chiang Mai wedge, Hua Hin fast-follow. Every page below is persona-led, not city-led for city's sake.

Tags: **[FACT]** verified demand/source · **[ASSUMPTION]** inference · **[REC]** recommendation. Legal-sensitive pieces are marked **[LEGAL-RISK]** and must carry "verify with official source / a licensed professional" framing and a last-updated date.

---

## Content strategy in one page

- **Own the DTV moment.** The newest, fastest-moving, highest-search-volume topic — banking exclusion, the 180-day tax clock, embassy document requirements — is under-served by neutral (non-lawyer) content.
- **Win with tools, not just articles.** Calculators and trackers attract links, capture emails, and are the moat vs. lawyer blogs and Facebook groups.
- **Chiang Mai for traffic, Hua Hin for differentiation.** Chiang Mai carries the nomad search volume; Hua Hin is the low-competition retiree/escape answer.
- **Monetise after trust, not before.** Lead with free tools/checklists; monetise insurance + connectivity affiliates early, visa/legal lead-gen and a paid pack once the email list exists.
- **Freshness is a feature.** Date-stamp everything; visa/tax/banking rules change yearly (2024–26 prove it).

- Pages **1–4 + 6 + 9** capture the highest-intent DTV/insurance searches and the two biggest 2026 pain points (banking, tax) within weeks.

## SEO principles & content gaps to exploit

**Confirmed gaps (weak/biased/missing today):**
1. Neutral (non-lawyer) DTV banking and tax tooling.
2. A working 180-day tax-residency tracker (concept explained, not toolised).
3. Hua Hin depth (a fraction of Chiang Mai/Bangkok/Phuket content).
4. Persona × city × budget fit ("remote worker, $X budget, asthma, dog → Y area").
5. Retiree insurance reality after 65 and the visa/insurance dependency.
6. Burning-season as an actionable, costed migration plan.
7. Dated, maintained freshness (most guides are stale or sales-led).

**On-page / technical rules:**
- Target question-style H2s (People Also Ask format) for the themes in §7.1 of the main report.
- Every legal/financial claim links the **primary official source** (see source-map CSV) and carries a last-updated date + disclaimer.
- Tools are indexable, fast, mobile-first, and work without sign-up (email gate on *results/export*, not entry).
- Cities interlink: Chiang Mai → burning-season escape → Hua Hin; city pages → cost calculator → visa comparator.
- Capture email on every tool; one welcome sequence ("5 mistakes in your first 30 days").
