## Journey A — Relocating family (primary)

### Phase 0 — Before arrival (decision & planning, ~6–3 months out)
**Situation:** A family (often a remote-working or entrepreneurial couple, not a fully corporate-relocated package) has decided on Bangkok but hasn't committed. The biggest irreversible decision is **school**; housing and visas follow.

**Key tasks**
- Choose an international school/curriculum and start applications (admission, testing, waitlists — can run 6–12 months). [FACT: fees ~280k–1.2m THB/yr; ISB KG–G5 ≈ 1.015m]
- Build an honest total budget: school + rent + insurance + transport + domestic help + utilities.
- Shortlist neighborhoods by **school commute** (Sukhumvit, Sathorn, Nichada Thani, Bang Na) and BTS/MRT access.
- Sort visas for the whole family (guardian/dependent, work or DTV/LTR for the earners). [LEGAL-RISK]
- Plan pet import (strict, long lead time) and shipment of belongings.

**Top pains**
- School fee shock and opaque "all-in" costs (levies, bus, lunch, enrolment).
- Not knowing which neighborhoods are realistic for a given school with Bangkok traffic.
- Contradictory/outdated visa advice; coordinating dependent paperwork.
- No single trusted source tying school → home → budget → visa together.

**What ThailandMate provides**
- School-fee & all-in-cost calculator; family cost-of-living calculator.
- **Neighborhood-by-school commute matcher** (BTS/MRT + school-bus vs car).
- Relocation timeline/checklist (what to apply for, in what order).
- Dependent/guardian visa checklist (links to thaievisa.go.th / immigration.go.th).
- Vetted lead capture: "get introduced to an admission advisor / family-insurance broker / rental agent."

### Phase 1 — First week (arrival & orientation)
**Situation:** Family lands; jet-lagged, kids present. They need to feel safe and functional fast.

**Key tasks**
- Complete arrival formalities: TDAC (done pre-arrival), passport/visa check, temporary address.
- Get SIMs/eSIM for adults; set up phones; install Grab/Bolt, LINE, LINE MAN, Google/transit maps.
- Temporary accommodation (serviced apartment/Airbnb) while house-hunting.
- Register address (TM30 via landlord/hotel) [LEGAL-RISK]; open the **bank account path** the visa allows.
- Locate nearest good hospital/clinic and pharmacy; ensure family insurance works; know emergency numbers.
- Get a Rabbit/MRT card; learn the BTS/MRT-to-school run.

**Top pains**
- TM30/address registration confusion and the **bank-account reality** (which visa is accepted; DTV/tourist excluded). [FACT]
- Transport overwhelm; choosing where to stay temporarily vs long-term.
- Healthcare confidence — where to take a sick child, will insurance pay.
- SIM/connectivity and money-handling friction before a Thai account exists.

**What ThailandMate provides**
- Arrival-week checklist (TDAC/TM30, SIM/eSIM, apps, transit cards, hospital selection).
- Banking-by-visa guide + interim money options (eSIM-first, transfer services, ATMs). [LEGAL-RISK]
- Family healthcare map (Bumrungrad/Samitivej/Bangkok Hospital; pediatric care; direct billing).
- Connectivity guide (eSIM/VPN affiliate).
- "First week" chat/FAQ and checklist inside the paid planner.

### Phase 2 — First month (settle the big commitments)
**Situation:** Temporary accommodation is costing premium rates; the family needs long-term housing, school finalised, and routines.

**Key tasks**
- Confirm school placement and pay; arrange school bus vs driver/car.
- Sign a **long-term lease near school**; check deposit terms, TM30-ready landlord, facilities.
- Set up home: fiber internet, electricity/water, appliances, air purifiers (air-quality days).
- Open Thai bank accounts (once on a qualifying visa); set bill payments.
- Register for 90-day reporting if applicable; understand family visa extensions. [LEGAL-RISK]
- Hire domestic help/nanny/cleaner; find kids' activities, grocery, pediatrician/dentist.
- Get transport sorted: BTS/MRT pass vs car + driver vs lease.

**Top pains**
- Deposit/contract pitfalls and "farang pricing"; landlords who won't file TM30.
- Home internet/utility setup; air quality for children.
- Banking/document round trips; making sense of compliance deadlines.
- Trustworthy domestic help; kids' social integration.

**What ThailandMate provides**
- Rental/lease checklist + red-flag guide; TM30-ready housing info.
- Vetted property-agent leads (commute-matched).
- 90-day/report reminder and compliance checklist (links official portals).
- Family services directory: domestic help, clinics/dentists, kids' activities (sponsored-listing/lead model).
- Home setup + air-quality guide (Air4Thai link).

### Phase 3 — By day 90 (integrated & optimising)
**Situation:** Functional and stable; now optimising cost, community and the long-term plan.

**Key tasks**
- Complete first 90-day report if required; calendar future extensions/renewals. [LEGAL-RISK]
- Confirm insurance renewals and direct-billing relationships; long-term health planning.
- Reassess commute/housing vs actual traffic; consider lease renewal vs move.
- Build community: school parent network, expat groups, spouse/partner networks.
- Plan visa runway (renewal, LTR/Privilege upgrade for high earners) and any tax position. [LEGAL-RISK]
- Evaluate longer horizon: stay vs renew; tax residency (180-day) if working remotely.

**Top pains**
- Compliance/renewal anxiety; tax-residency questions for remote-earning parents.
- Spouse/partner isolation; community building.
- Long-term visa and money strategy (LTR vs DTV vs Privilege; bringing money in).

**What ThailandMate provides**
- 180-day tax-residency tracker + plain-English explainer (general info only). [LEGAL-RISK]
- Visa runway/renewal planner; vetted tax/legal lead capture.
- Renewal reminders; community/events pointers; "second 90 days" resources.
- Cross-check that setup decisions matched real costs (budget vs actual).

## Journey B — Digital nomad / remote worker (secondary)

### Phase 0 — Before arrival (~2–6 weeks out)
**Situation:** Booked flights to Bangkok; needs a legal basis to stay and a productive landing pad.
- **Tasks:** choose visa (DTV vs LTR vs visa-exempt); prepare 500k THB proof + work documents for DTV; book short-term coliving/condo (Sukhumvit/On Nut/Ari/Phra Khanong); buy travel/health insurance; eSIM/VPN.
- **Pains:** DTV document/seasoning paranoia; **banking exclusion**; uncertainty about neighborhoods and internet; tax-residency (180-day) blind spot. [FACT/LEGAL-RISK]
- **ThailandMate:** visa comparator + DTV checklist; 180-day tax tracker; neighborhood/cost guides; insurance & eSIM affiliates; coliving links.

### Phase 1 — First week
- **Tasks:** TDAC; TM30 via accommodation; SIM/eSIM; Grab/Bolt/LINE; set up work base (coworking such as The Hive, apartment internet); locate clinics; interim money via foreign cards/transfer services.
- **Pains:** no Thai bank account (DTV); choosing a coworking/café base; noise/commute; staying legal on reporting.
- **ThailandMate:** arrival-week checklist; banking-by-visa guide + workarounds; connectivity guide; neighborhood map; healthcare basics.

### Phase 2 — First month
- **Tasks:** extend vs plan exit before 180-day tax threshold; find long-term condo (lease, deposit, TM30); open a qualifying account if visa allows; home internet; 90-day report logic; routine (gym, laundry, transport card).
- **Pains:** lease/deposit traps; the 180-day/remittance decision; coworking vs home; staying on top of compliance.
- **ThailandMate:** rental checklist; tax tracker; TM30/90-day guide; condo-agent leads; budget calculator.

### Phase 3 — By day 90
- **Tasks:** decide extend-in-country or border reset to manage days/tax; evaluate Bangkok vs Chiang Mai/Hua Hin (cost, pollution, community); renew insurance; plan longer-term visa (LTR if high-income).
- **Pains:** the tax-residency/remittance math; air-quality days; decision between cities; banking remains hard.
- **ThailandMate:** visa runway + day-count tool; city comparison; LTR info; insurance renewal; tax/legal lead capture.

## Cross-audience principles
- **Decision-gate logic:** families' biggest gate is **school** (pre-arrival); nomads' is **visa + banking** (pre-arrival/week one). Product surfaces each at the right moment.
- **Tools first, lead-gen second:** calculators/trackers/checklists do the self-service job; high-ticket/high-risk needs route to **vetted, licensed** partners with disclosure.
- **Every [LEGAL-RISK] step** links the official portal and says "general information — verify with the official source / a licensed professional," with a last-updated date (see `thailandmate-source-register.csv`).
