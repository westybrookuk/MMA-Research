# Bangkok Partner & Affiliate Readiness — ThailandMate (AbroadMate)

**Date:** 28 August 2026
**Purpose:** map the practical availability of potential partner categories for the Bangkok MVP, so monetisation can be tested **without** prematurely claiming approval, publishing commission rates, or presenting any provider as "vetted."

**Tags:** [FACT] existence/publicly-stated partner programs · [ASSUMPTION] fit/availability inference · [REC] recommendation.
**Hard rules applied:**
- We have **not** signed up for or been approved by any program. All entries are `unverified` until an application is completed and terms confirmed.
- **Commission/lead-fee rates are never published** in user-facing content; rates are stored privately after confirmation.
- No provider is described as "vetted/trusted/recommended" until independently verified (licence, terms, reliability).
- Regulated categories (insurance, visa/legal) are **signposts to licensed providers**, never advice, and always carry a disclosure + official-source link.

## Status legend
- `verification_status`: **unverified** (not applied/checked) · **program_exists** (public affiliate/partner page found) · **applied** · **approved** · **declined**.
- `affiliate_or_lead_gen`: affiliate · lead-gen · sponsored · none-yet.
- `disclosure_needed`: yes/no (affiliate/lead/sponsor relationship disclosed to users).

## Findings & recommendations

**Fastest to test (programmatic, low-risk, day-one candidates) [REC]:**
1. **eSIM** (Airalo — public affiliate page found) and **VPN** (NordVPN/Surfshark — public affiliate programs). Low regulation, global, first-week purchase, easy fit with the connectivity checklist.
2. **Money transfer** (Wise — referral/partner exists) directly serves the documented DTV banking pain; keep framing as "options," not banking advice.
3. **Nomad travel/health insurance** (SafetyWing-type) — relevant and programmatic, **but medium risk**: clearly disclose, never claim it satisfies Thai visa-insurance requirements, and route long-stay/retirement insurance to OIC-listed, vetted brokers only.

**Lead-gen to build later (higher value, needs vetting/agreements) [REC]:**
- **Coliving/serviced apartments, coworking, rental/relocation agents** — high relevance to housing-first checklists; negotiate per-lead/referral after the product has traffic; verify each provider before listing.
- **Licensed visa/legal and O-A insurance brokers** — highest ticket but **highest regulated risk**; require licensed partners, formal agreements, disclosure, and a legal review before any referral link goes live.

**Operational rules [REC]:**
- Keep a private partner tracker with application status, confirmed (unpublished) terms, and licence references; the public site shows only a disclosure badge, never rates.
- Default every provider card to `verification_status: unverified`; it becomes `vetted` only after a documented check.
- Regulated cards (insurance/visa/legal) always render the "general information, not advice — verify with the official source / a licensed professional" notice.

## Facts / assumptions / recommendations
- **Facts [FACT]** (28 Aug 2026): public affiliate pages confirmed for Airalo (help center + affiliates@airalo.com); SafetyWing, NordVPN/Surfshark and Wise operate public partner/referral programs; OIC/tgia is the official Thai long-stay insurance reference; AIS/True are the mobile/fibre operators.
- **Assumptions [ASSUMPTION]:** local operator/coworking/coliving/agent willingness to pay for leads; conversion strength of each category; VPN neutral-use framing adequacy (to confirm with legal).
- **Recommendations [REC]:** test eSIM + VPN first, money-transfer second, nomad insurance third (carefully); defer high-ticket visa/legal and O-A insurance lead-gen until partners are vetted and legal-reviewed; never publish rates or claim approval/vetting prematurely.
