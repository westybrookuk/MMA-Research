# ThailandMate — Source-Risk Audit (high-risk claims)

**Date:** 28 August 2026
**Scope:** the visa, tax, banking, healthcare and insurance claims made across the ThailandMate research (`thailandmate-opportunity-report.md` and related). Purpose: before any of this is published by an AI-assisted product, classify each high-risk claim, state whether it is safe to publish, and identify the primary source and review interval.
**Rule applied:** legal/tax/visa/insurance statements are **general information only**, link the primary official source, show a last-verified date, and never constitute personalised advice. Secondary/anecdotal sourcing is **not** sufficient to publish a rule as definitive.
**Tiers:** **Official** (government/regulator) · **Primary-secondary** (licensed firms/banks/official industry bodies; reputable but commercial) · **Secondary** (media/guides) · **Anecdotal** (forums/commentary).

## 2. Publishing rules derived from the audit

1. **Never publish a visa/tax/banking/insurance rule from a secondary source alone.** Each rule page must link the **primary official source** (thaievisa.go.th, ltr.boi.go.th, rd.go.th, oic.or.th/longstay.tgia.org, the relevant bank, thailandprivilege.co.th, dbd.go.th) and show a last-verified date. The full primary-source set is in `thailandmate-source-map.csv` / `thailandmate-source-register.csv` with change frequency and risk level.
2. **High-risk tax (4) and business-setup (14, 15) claims** are explainer/background only — always paired with "consult a licensed professional" and (for tax) the Revenue Department; no tool output should read as advice.
3. **Banking (6):** quote only the bank whose published terms you cite; phrase others as "generally, as of <date>"; review quarterly.
4. **Embassy-specific figures (1, 2, 8, 12):** national figures are floors; fees/insurance/seasoning vary by post — always route the reader to *their* embassy/consulate.
5. **Do not publish unconfirmed items (17)** beyond an explicit "rumoured/not confirmed" note; suppress any high-risk claim whose `Claim.publish_status ≠ ok` (see global architecture §5).
6. **Facts vs framing:** statistics like the 35k DTV figure (3) and AQI (18) are fine when **attributed and dated**; they must not be presented as official Thailand counts (no official nomad census exists).
7. **Refresh cadence:** high-risk rules quarterly (immediately on announced change); Privilege promos and bank policy quarterly; LTR/school fees semi-annually/annually; live data (AQI/FX) via feed.

## 3. Facts vs assumptions vs recommendations
- **Facts [FACT]:** the source tiers and URLs above (accessed 28 Aug 2026); which claims currently have an official vs secondary basis.
- **Assumptions [ASSUMPTION]:** that quarterly review is sufficient to catch most changes; that embedding official links + dates materially reduces liability (reasonable but not legal certainty).
- **Recommendations [REC]:** adopt the claim-tier gating and review cadence in the content model (`global-product-architecture.md` §5–6); have a qualified Thai lawyer/tax adviser review high-risk pages before launch; treat every number here as "verify at publish time."
- **Disclaimer:** this audit is an editorial-risk process, not legal advice.
