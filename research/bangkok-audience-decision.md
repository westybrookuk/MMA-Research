## 1. Weighted scorecard — Bangkok, self-service product

Scores 1–5 (5 = best). For risk and maintenance, **5 = lowest risk / lowest burden**. Weights reflect a **self-service digital launch with no existing brand or partner supply chain**.

| Criterion | Weight | Remote workers / nomads | Families | Retirees | Workers / founders |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| User pain (first-90-day urgency) | 15% | 4 | **5** | 4 | 4 |
| Discoverability (search + communities) | 15% | **5** | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Willingness to pay (self-service) | 15% | 3 | **4** | 3 | 2 |
| Affiliate potential (programmatic, day-one) | 10% | **5** | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| Lead-generation potential (high-ticket) | 10% | 3 | **5** | **5** | **5** |
| Ease of serving with a self-service product | 15% | **5** | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| Trust / legal / safety margin (5 = lowest risk) | 10% | 3 | **4** | 2 | 1 |
| Maintenance ease (5 = lowest burden) | 10% | 3 | **4** | 2 | 2 |
| **Weighted total** | 100% | **3.95** | **3.85** | 3.00 | 2.50 |

*Remote workers: .60+.75+.45+.50+.30+.75+.30+.30 = 3.95 · Families: .75+.45+.60+.30+.50+.45+.40+.40 = 3.85.*

**Read of the result [REC]:** the two audiences are close for good reason — they monetise differently. **Remote workers win the self-service wedge** because they are cheap to reach at zero brand, their problems are procedural and repeatable (visa → banking → connectivity → housing → transit → compliance → day-counting), and they convert through programmatic **affiliate** products that exist on day one with no sales team. **Families score higher on willingness-to-pay and lead-generation**, but that value is relationship-driven (property agents, schools, insurance brokers) a new brand cannot transact immediately; the school/housing decision is also partly relational/offline. Families are therefore the **strategic secondary and the high-LTV layer**, not the first self-service purchase.

## 2. Recommendations

- **[REC] Primary Bangkok audience: digital nomads / remote workers (DTV).**
- **[REC] Secondary audience: self-funded/DIY relocating families.** Build family tools (international-school fee calculator, school↔neighborhood commute matcher) as the lead-gen wedge once the brand and email list exist.
- **[REC] Exclude from the first self-service paid product:**
  - **Retirees** — highest health/insurance and tax-trust risk; prefer human brokers; serve later via vetted insurance/property lead-gen, never a DIY health/legal guide.
  - **Workers / founders** — company setup is regulated professional work (Foreign Business Act, work permits); **never** a self-service legal product; route high-ticket leads to vetted law/accounting firms only.
- **[REC] Corporate-relocated families are also out of scope** (handled by relocation companies); target DIY/self-funded families.

### 2.1 First paid product
**"Bangkok First 90 Days" — a self-service planner for remote workers**: current, Bangkok-specific, step-by-step system covering visa choice/documents, banking-by-visa-status (+ interim money), SIM/eSIM/fiber/VPN, neighborhood + safe renting, BTS/MRT/airport setup, health insurance & clinics, TDAC/TM30/90-day compliance, and a **180-day tax-residency tracker** — as checklists, decision tools and a short verified-provider list.

**Problem it solves [FACT anchors]:** DTV holders are excluded from major-bank accounts ([Kasikorn terms](https://www.kasikornbank.com/en/personal/account/pages/savings.aspx), 28 Aug 2026); a 180-day tax-residency/2024-remittance rule creates a day-counting trap ([Chiang Rai Times](https://www.chiangraitimes.com/finance/thailand-digital-nomad-tax-rule/), [LEGAL-RISK]); DTV drew 35,000+ applications in year one ([IMI Daily](https://www.imidaily.com/asia-pacific/thailands-digital-nomad-visa-surpasses-35000-applicants-in-its-first-year/)); official rules live on [thaievisa.go.th](https://www.thaievisa.go.th/) and [immigration.go.th](https://www.immigration.go.th/).

### 2.3 First three acquisition channels
1. **Tool-led organic search (SEO)** — free high-intent tools (visa comparator, 180-day tracker, banking checker, cost calculator) capture pre-arrival Google traffic and earn links; email-gate results/export, not the tool.
2. **Reddit + Facebook communities** — r/digitalnomad, r/Thailand, Bangkok nomad/expat groups; value-first, mod-approved.
3. **Coworking / coliving / serviced-apartment partnerships** — referral codes and newcomer offers in Sukhumvit spaces (e.g. The Hive-type venues) and coliving operators.
*(Then: short-form video; nomad newsletter/partner co-marketing + email nurture.)*

### 2.4 First five monetisation categories
1. **Travel / health insurance** — nomad travel-medical affiliate; (retiree/family) broker lead-gen later. [insurance compliance — link oic.or.th / longstay.tgia.org]
2. **Connectivity** — eSIM + VPN affiliate (+ home fiber); high-frequency first-week purchase.
3. **Housing** — coliving / serviced-apartment affiliate and condo/rental-agent lead-gen.
4. **Money transfer** — cross-border transfer referral (directly serves the DTV banking pain).
5. **Visa / legal & property lead-gen** — vetted, licensed partners for DTV extensions, retirement, and (family) property; **[LEGAL-RISK]** disclose relationships; never present as regulated advice.
