## 1. Exact and variant-name searches (sector use)

Variants checked: **Shift Shores / ShiftShores / Shifting Shores / ShiftingShores** and **Land & Launch / LandAndLaunch / Land and Launch / Launch & Land / LaunchAndLand / LandLaunch**.

### Shift Shores
- **No relocation / immigration / expat-service product named "Shift Shores" or "ShiftShores" was found.** Exact-name searches did not surface a living-abroad platform.
- **"Shifting Shores" is, however, an established phrase tied to coastal/climate change:**
  - The **National Trust (UK)** runs a programme/report series called **"Shifting Shores"** about coastal erosion and a changing coastline (`into.org` PDF; National Trust strategy).
  - A **Sciences Po / City of Paris** funded academic project **"Shifting Shores" (2019–2022)** studied Mediterranean river-delta coastal change (`shiftshores.hypotheses.org`).
- Travel-media noise exists around the words: podcasts such as **SHIFT (A Travel Local Collective)**, **The ShiftShapers Podcast**, and the travel-news brand **Skift** — "shift/shores" is a fairly crowded travel-editorial soundscape.
- **Semantic point (material to a world-wide platform):** "**shores**" means coastal land. A platform whose roadmap includes inland/mountain cities (Chiang Mai, Mexico City, Madrid, Berlin, São Paulo) is poorly served by a name that literally denotes the coast (see §4 and naming test in §5).

### Land & Launch
- **No relocation/expat platform named "Land & Launch" was found**, but the phrase space is noisy:
  - **`launchandland.com` is an active business — "Launch + Land", a Minneapolis (US) web-design/development agency** building small-business websites (Webflow; "$8–30k builds"). This is the concrete conflict: reverse-order name, and **web development (class 42-adjacent)** is exactly the sector our SaaS/tools sit near.
  - "**land and launch**" phrasing is heavily used in **aerospace/rocket news** (e.g. LandSpace's "launch and landing" attempts) and in **land-investing / land-flipping coaching** ("land investing masterclass", landinvestor.co) — property/education (classes 36/41) noise.
  - Condensed variant **`landlaunch.com`** is registered (parked/GoDaddy); **`shiftingshores.com`** is parked on Sedo.

**Best use of each name:**
- **Shift Shores** — best as a **newsletter / editorial column / campaign** (it is travel-magazine in tone) or a coastal-destination sub-brand. Weak as the global parent because of "shores" (coastal-only), the climate/erosion "Shifting Shores" association, and awkward Thai pronunciation.
- **Land & Launch** — best as a **product / programme name** (e.g. an arrival sprint or launch programme), not a place brand. As a *parent* it collides with a live web-dev agency (class 42), is noisy in land-investing/rocket contexts, and reads like an agency rather than a relocation platform.

## 5. Naming examples — both patterns, tested on real cities

Test of `[Brand]: [Place]` vs `[Place] [Brand]` across coastal **and inland** destinations.

|---|---|---|
| Bangkok | Shift Shores: Bangkok | Bangkok Shift Shores |
| Chiang Mai (inland) | Shift Shores: Chiang Mai — **"shores" is wrong, no coast** | Chiang Mai Shift Shores — **contradictory** |
