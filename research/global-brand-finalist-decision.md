## 3. Recommendation

- **Working brand (best balance of clarity + brandability): 🥇 LocalFooting.** Cleanest commercial picture: every TLD likely free, no in-sector conflict, strong metaphor ("get your footing" = establish yourself confidently), natural editions, easy Thai pronunciation, ownable as a composite mark. **Adopt LocalFooting as the working brand now** (internal use / migration prep) while commissioning clearance.
- **Strongest distinctiveness-first: 🥈 CityOrienteer.** Most memorable/ownable if the professional search clears the navigation-app overlap; keep as the first fallback.
- **Safest clarity-first: 🥉 NewHereCo.** Instantly understood and best Thai, but weakest trademark and a taken YouTube handle — use only if LocalFooting and CityOrienteer both stall.
- **FreshWeeks:** keep as a **product/campaign name** (e.g. an onboarding series), not the parent.

**Risk note:** LocalFooting's only residual risk is the distant engineering use of "footing" — different sector and different mark overall, so low, but the professional search should still confirm classes 35/39/41/42. Nothing here constitutes clearance.
