# ThailandMate — Bangkok Validation Plan (7-Day, Low-Cost)

**Date:** 28 August 2026
**Tests:** the decision in `bangkok-audience-decision.md` — primary audience = **remote workers/nomads**, first product = **"Bangkok First 90 Days" self-service planner**, with families as the secondary/high-LTV audience.
**Tags:** [ASSUMPTION] all thresholds/prices are hypotheses · [LEGAL-RISK] official-source framing required. No traffic, commission or competitor-price figures are invented.
**Budget:** < ~US$100 (landing tool, email tool, optional micro-boost; presale refundable). **No finished product needed** — presale/waitlist validates demand.

---

## 1. Twelve customer interview questions

Target **12 × 20–25 min interviews in 7 days**: ~8 remote workers/nomads (pre-arrival or first 6 months) and ~4 families (to validate the secondary wedge). Behaviour, not opinions; no pitching.

**Remote workers / nomads (primary):**
1. What stage are you at — researching Bangkok, visa in progress, just arrived, or first 6 months?
2. What brought you to Bangkok over Chiang Mai or the islands?
3. Walk me through your visa decision (DTV / LTR / visa-exempt / other) and what was most confusing.
4. What happened when you tried to open a Thai bank account — which visa, which bank, any refusals or workarounds?
5. How are you handling the 180-day tax-residency rule and bringing income into Thailand? *([LEGAL-RISK] — listen, do not advise.)*
6. What did your **first week** actually involve — SIM/internet, money, where to stay, getting around — and what broke?
7. How did you choose where to live (neighborhood, rent, lease, deposit), and what surprised you?
8. What did you spend money on in the first 90 days (insurance, eSIM, coliving/agent, coworking, transfers)? Which was worth it?
9. What question could you **not** find a reliable, current answer to?
10. If one current, Bangkok-specific "first 90 days" planner (checklists + banking/connectivity/housing steps + a 180-day tracker) had existed, would you have bought it — and what would you expect to pay? *(Let them name a price.)*

**Families (secondary validation):**
11. How did you choose your kids' school and decide where to live relative to it? What did it cost and what surprised you?
12. What stressed you most in the first 90 days — and would you pay more for a family planner with a school-fee calculator and a school↔neighborhood commute matcher, or prefer referrals to vetted agents/advisors?

**Score each interview for:** (a) an acute, recent, recurring mistake; (b) evidence of prior spend; (c) the exact deliverable they'd pay for; (d) nomad vs family vs overlap (remote-working parents).

## 2. Three landing-page messages to test (A/B/C)

Same page, one variable = the **headline**; all include: free tool lead-magnet (180-day tax tracker **or** banking-by-visa checker), three planner bullet outcomes, presale price button, a "refer me to a vetted person" button, and a sources/last-updated trust line.

- **Message A — pain/change (nomad fear hook):**
  **"DTV nomads are getting turned away by Thai banks now. Here's exactly what to do in your first 90 days in Bangkok."**
- **Message B — outcome/completeness (nomad reassurance):**
  **"Land in Bangkok with a plan: the right visa, banking that works, where to live, and every first-week setup step — sorted."**
- **Message C — family/secondary (measure separately):**
  **"Moving to Bangkok with kids? Get the school, home near school, budget and healthcare right before you land."**

Track per variant: email-capture rate, presale clicks, referral-lead clicks. Message C doubles as the family-secondary test.

## 3. The 7-day experiment

**Set-up (Day 0):** one-page landing (Carrd/Webflow/Notion), free tool (a real or polished-no-code 180-day tracker / banking-by-visa checker), email capture (free tier), presale checkout (Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy, refundable), Calendly for interviews. Link only official sources.

| Day | Actions |
|---|---|
| **1** | Publish landing + free tool + presale. Post Message variants A/B to **two** high-intent communities (one nomad subreddit/Discord, one Bangkok nomad Facebook group); ask for 20-min interviews in exchange for the free toolkit. |
| **2** | Post Message C to one family/parent group (secondary test). Begin interviews. Add the landing link to personal/professional profiles; post one value-first answer in r/Thailand. |
| **3** | Publish one short-form clip (banking-by-visa reality) with tool link; cross-post to coworking/coliving Slack/Discord. Continue interviews (target 4–6 done). |
| **4** | Send the first email to captured signups ("5 mistakes in your first 30 days"); ask recipients who bought/reserved for a quick interview. Optional micro-boost (≤US$30) to the winning headline so far. |
| **5** | Rotate Message A/B to one more community and a nomad newsletter/partner co-post. Interviews target 8–10. |
| **6** | Reach out to 5 candidate partners (1 insurer/broker, 1 eSIM/VPN, 1 coliving/serviced-apt, 1 condo agent, 1 visa agent) to test lead-gen willingness. |
| **7** | Tally metrics; run go/no-go; record top pains, unprompted price answers, audience split, best headline, best channel. |

## 4. Measurable success criteria (7 days)

**All are starting hypotheses [ASSUMPTION].**

| Metric | Threshold (7 days) | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| Landing visitors | **400–600+** | Organic distribution reach |
| Email sign-ups (free tool) | **80–150+** (~10–15% capture) | Problem/tool resonance |
| Customer interviews | **8–12** (nomad-leaning) | Qualitative validation |
| **Planner pre-orders / paid reservations** | **5–10+** | Actual willingness to pay (watch $29 vs $49 choice) |
| Affiliate clicks (insurance / eSIM / VPN) | **50+** tracked | Day-one affiliate intent |
| Vetted-referral / provider enquiries | **3+** | Lead-gen demand (nomad + family) |
| Partner lead-gen interest | **2+ positive replies** | Future lead-gen supply |

**Go / no-go [REC]:**
- **Green — build the nomad "First 90 Days" planner:** ≥80 emails **and** ≥5 pre-orders **and** interviews naming the same top pains (banking, compliance, tax days). Then fast-follow the family tools (school-fee calculator + commute matcher) as the lead-gen layer.
- **Amber — tools win, product doesn't yet:** strong email/tool and affiliate-click traction but few pre-orders → ship free tools + affiliate content for cash flow/list growth; re-test the paid offer (price, bundling, or family edition).
- **Red — no traction:** weak emails and no pre-orders → reassess the angle (headline) or audience before investing in the planner.

**Guardrails:** general information only on visa/tax/insurance ([LEGAL-RISK]) — link thaievisa.go.th, immigration.go.th, rd.go.th, oic.or.th and show a last-updated date; never promise approvals/account openings; do not state commission rates or unverified competitor pricing; refund presales if the planner ships late.
