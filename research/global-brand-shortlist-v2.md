**Date:** 29 August 2026.
**What this is:** a preliminary screen. **It is NOT legal trademark clearance and must not be described as such.** DNS results are from Google public DNS-over-HTTPS (no NS/SOA = *likely* free; never a registrar guarantee). ## 1. Scoring method (1–5; 5 = best)

| Column | Meaning (5 = best) |
|---|---|
| clarity | Instantly understandable / hints at relocation-living |
| distinctiveness | Not generic; stands out in a search |
| global_pronunciation | Easy in English, Thai and other major languages; no awkward consonant clusters or mis-pronunciations |
| destination_edition_fit | Natural to extend to city/country editions |
| domain_signal | 5 = primary TLDs return no records (likely available); 1 = .com taken |
| social_handle_signal | 5 = probed handle free; 1 = found taken; 3 = couldn't read (platform block) |
| conflict_risk_safe | 5 = no in-sector conflict found; 1 = strong conflict |
| trademark_registrability | 5 = coined/arbitrary (likely registrable after clearance); 1 = descriptive/generic or clearly blocked |

> Every single name, including the front-runners, **requires professional clearance** before use. `conflict_risk_safe` and `trademark_registrability` score *likelihood*, not legal outcome.

| 🥇 Best balance | **LocalFooting** | compound | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 |

All three returned **no NS/SOA records across `.com`, `.io`, `.app`, `.co`, `.co.uk`** on 2026-08-29 (recheck at registrar). YouTube handles `@localfooting` and `@cityorienteer` returned 404 (free at check); `@newhereco` resolves to an existing unrelated channel ("I'm New Here").

### LocalFooting — *best balance* — **recommended default**
- **Pronunciation:** English: **"LOH-kǝl FOO-ting"** (like "local footing"). Thai: easy — **โลคัล ฟุตติ้ง** / *lo-kan fut-ting*; English "-ing" is widely used in Thai, final `-ing` renders naturally; no hard clusters.
- **Tagline (one line):** *"Get your footing in a new city."*
- **Why:** "Footing" = a secure, established position; "local" = where you're living. Implies settling-in and confidence without any banned root, works for tools, guides, paid packs **and** a provider directory, and extends cleanly.
- Editions: **Bangkok Footing** / *localfooting.com/bangkok* · **Lisbon Footing** · **Mexico City Footing**.
- Watch: `-ing` spelling in some markets; professional TM check still required.

### CityOrienteer — *strongest distinctiveness*
- **Pronunciation:** English: **"SIT-ee aw-ree-uhn-TEER"** (one who orienteers / navigates). Thai: **ซิตี้ ออเรียนเทียร** / *or-ri-an-tia*; 3 syllables, `-eer` softens to `-ia/-aa` — understandable, slightly longer.
- **Tagline:** *"Your compass for a new city."*
- **Why:** Metaphor of *navigating your new city* is ownable and memorable; highly distinctive mark. Watch-outs: the bare word "orienteer" is used by an unrelated US music-PR firm (orienteer.us) and an outdoor navigation app (Barleycorn; orienteer.co) — different sectors but the navigation-app overlap merits a professional look.
- Editions: **Bangkok Orienteer** / *cityorienteer.com/bangkok* · **Lisbon Orienteer** · **Mexico City Orienteer** (also "Orienteer Lisbon" reads well).

### NewHereCo — *safest clarity-first*
- **Pronunciation:** English: **"nyoo–HEER–koh"** (New Here Co.). Thai: **นิวเฮียร์ โค** / *niu-hia koh* — very easy.
- **Tagline:** *"You're new here. We help with the rest."*
- **Why:** Instantly clear and warm; "Co." signals company/collective. Watch-outs: the phrase "new here" is common (weaker, more descriptive mark; YouTube `@newhereco` is already used by an unrelated channel) — likely the **weakest trademark** of the three and the one most likely to need a logo/wordmark treatment to protect.
- Editions: **New in Bangkok** / *newhere.co/bangkok* · **New in Lisbon** · **New in Mexico City** (the edition phrasing "New in [city]" is the strongest fit of all three).

**If only one is pursued:** **LocalFooting** — it balances meaning, availability, pronunciation (including Thai), and edition flexibility; run the professional TM search on LocalFooting **and** CityOrienteer in parallel before buying.

## 3. The other preliminary candidate

- **FreshWeeks** (compound) — TLDs mostly clear; evokes the first weeks in a place. Marked preliminary but slightly product/programme-like and less natural as a parent company; good as a *campaign/product* name (e.g. a "Fresh Weeks" onboarding series) rather than the umbrella brand.

## 4. Rejected candidates (and why)

- **Relocadi** (coined) — DNS-clean but **too close to Relocity**, a major VC-backed relocation-management SaaS (relocity.com; Fortune-500 clients, app) and **Relocify** (NL relocation startup). The "reloc-" root is crowded; risk of confusion in the exact sector. *Do not pursue.*
- **Dwello, Arrivo, Lumio, Kova, Rova, Tula** (coined) — primary `.com`/`.io` registered and/or existing products (e.g. Arrivo = Italian "arrival" + travel brands; Tula = established consumer brand/word; Dwello resembles proptech names). Coined single-word `.com` space is effectively saturated for pronounceable short names.
- **PlaceWise, NewGround** (compounds) — `.com` taken; "new ground" collides with Newgrounds media; "-ground" is also awkward in Thai.
- **LocalOrbit** — ambiguous DNS probe (rate-limited) and "orbit" is a well-used startup metaphor; parked as `unclear` / not worth front-running.
- **Suki, Cleo, Nova, Remy, Indi, Mara** (friendly/human) — **all rejected**: `.com`/`.io` taken, common given names/words with saturated social handles and weak (nearly unregistrable) marks. Specific notes: **Suki** in Thai (สุกี) is associated with suki hotpot and the word for "like/fancy" — confusing for a Thailand launch; Cleo/Nova are heavily used fintech/tech names. Bare first-name platforms also extend poorly to cities.

## 5. Naming hierarchy (applies to whichever finalist wins)

```
GLOBAL PARENT (company + platform + app):  LocalFooting / CityOrienteer / NewHereCo
DESTINATION EDITIONS (place + brand, not new words):
   Bangkok  → e.g. "Bangkok Footing"  (path: /bangkok)
   Lisbon   → "Lisbon Footing"
   Mexico City → "Mexico City Footing"
PRODUCTS (descriptive, under the parent):
   "Footing First 90 Days: Bangkok" · "First 7 Days checklist" · "Budget Planner" · "Footing Directory"
```
Own the **root** (trademark weight there); keep "First 90 Days", "checklist", "matcher", "directory" as descriptive product terms.

## 6. Pronunciation / Thai-language screen (finalists)
- Avoided `-nd/-g` final clusters (NewGround, Landing…) which Thai lacks; vowel/soft endings preferred.
- Thai has fluid L/R, no final V (use W), and `-ing` is natural via "-ิง". **LocalFooting** and **NewHereCo** pass cleanly; **CityOrienteer** passes with a longer, slightly anglicised feel.
- Checked for embarrassing Thai meanings: none of the three roots is a known Thai word; **"suki"** is one (reason for rejection).
