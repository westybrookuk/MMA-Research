# Bangkok First 90 Days — Paid-Pack Conversion Page Specification

**Status:** Implementation-ready content/UX specification. No website code is changed by this document; it is the copy and structure brief the site will be built to.
**Created:** 28 August 2026. Owner: content lead. Legal/privacy reviewer (⚖) must approve final copy before the page or any payment goes live (see `abroadmate-launch-signoff-checklist.md` block E).

> **Standing constraint [FACT of scope]:** This page sells a **research pack and checklist product** — organised, sourced, dated information for your own move. It must **not** imply, anywhere, that the product provides **visa, tax, banking, legal, medical, insurance, or property/letting-agent advice**. Every regulated topic inside the pack is a *verify-before-you-act signpost to official sources*, never a personalised recommendation.

9. **Final CTA band** repeating the early-access offer and the report-a-change link.

**Design rules:** one idea per section; CTA repeated after sections 3, 6 and 9; every figure on the page carries its date; no countdown timers or fake scarcity; no stock-photo "guarantee" badges.

## 3. Product promise (exact copy)

> **What Bangkok First 90 Days promises:** a single, organised pack that takes you from the airport to a settled first three months — week-by-week tasks, a realistic arrival-and-monthly budget, six neighbourhood deep-dives with dated rent ranges and station-stop counts, and the right official source for every visa, tax, banking, medical, insurance or housing question.
>
> **What it doesn't promise:** it isn't an immigration agent, accountant, lawyer, doctor, insurer or letting agent. It won't tell you *which* visa you qualify for, *whether* a tax rule applies to you, or *if* a flat or policy is right for you. It shows you where those answers officially live, what the current rules say as of the pack's date, and what to ask the qualified professional you choose. Information here is educational and time-stamped — not personalised advice.

## 4. Positioning to audience

### 4.1 Primary — remote workers / digital nomads (lead message)

> **Built around how remote workers actually move:** reliable internet and backup workspots before you need them, BTS/MRT station-stop counts (not guessed door-to-door minutes), rent bands per neighbourhood with their source dates, arrival costs split from monthly costs, and a week-by-week checklist so the first month isn't a scramble — while visa and tax specifics stay exactly where they belong: at the official sources and the professionals who advise on your situation.

Supporting bullets (remote-worker module flavour): connectivity per area, commute *stop counts*, monthly vs arrival budgeting, co-working/coffee-shop *research candidates* (clearly labelled unverified), SIM/connectivity signposts to the regulator and operators.

### 4.2 Secondary — self-funded families (one section, never diluting the lead)

> **Families: same sources, different priorities.** The pack flags what to research early — longer-stay accommodation patterns, school-place timelines, healthcare and insurance signposts to official sources, and quieter neighbourhoods to shortlist. It gives you dated ranges and the official channels to verify them; it doesn't choose schools, recommend insurers, or advise on family visas — it points you to the people and official pages who can.

### 4.3 "Not for you if…" strip (explicit, trust-building)

> This probably isn't for you if you want someone to tell you exactly which visa to apply for, which insurance policy to buy, or which flat to rent, or if you need legal, tax, medical or property advice about your own circumstances. That's what qualified professionals and official government sources are for — this pack gets you to the right ones faster.

## 5. Exact modules included in the paid pack

Each module is listed on the page with a one-line, honest description. Nothing here promises regulated advice.

1. **90-day week-by-week plan** — a checklist from 7 days out to day 90: what to arrange before flying, first-day tasks, first-week admin, month-one decisions, month-two/three settling. Built on the free First 7 Days checklist.
2. **Six neighbourhood deep-dives (On Nut, Phra Khanong, Ari/Phaya Thai, Bang Na, Central Sukhumvit, Sathorn–Silom)** — BTS/MRT station names and interchanges, stop counts to key hubs, **dated rent bands per dwelling type** shown as labelled estimates with source dates, convenience/noise/family context clearly marked as editorial impression (no invented scores), and a shortlist builder.
3. **Arrival-cost vs monthly-cost budget planner (extended)** — one-off arrival spend separated from recurring monthly spend, with a plug-in-the-rent calculator and the price-basket sources behind every line (rents, transit, groceries, SIM). *You* enter your own numbers; ranges are sourced and dated.
4. **Official-sources hub** — curated, dated links for immigration/visa, tax/revenue, banking, telecoms, health and insurance regulation, with a "what this page is for / what it can't tell you" note per source and a "verify before acting" flag. This is a directory of official pages, not advice.
5. **Document and decisions tracker** — a personal checklist of *your* open items (which visa route you're checking with whom, which viewings, which policy quotes), with blank fields for the answers you get from officials/professionals.
6. **First-week field kit** — airport-to-city options signposted to official operators, SIM/eSIM options signposted to operators/regulator, transport card basics, with safety nets and "confirm the current fare/hours" notes.
7. **Quarterly update notes** — subscribers to the pack edition get notified when the dated data is refreshed (rents quarterly, transit semi-annually, official links on change).
8. **"Report a change" access** — the correction workflow (`abroadmate-report-change-workflow.md`) so readers on the ground help keep it accurate.

**Explicitly NOT included (stated on the page):** personalised visa/tax/legal/medical/insurance determinations; property viewings, reservations or agent services; any guarantee of prices, availability, approvals or outcomes.

## 12. Objections and answers (on-page FAQ)

1. **"Is this just scraped listings I could find myself?"** — The listings are free to find; what you're paying for is the *organisation*: a 90-day plan, areas compared side by side, arrival costs split from monthly, stop counts instead of vague "20 minutes!" claims, and every source dated so you know what's stale. The raw basics stay free for that reason.
2. **"Will it tell me what visa to get?"** — No, and anything that promises that without knowing your situation shouldn't be trusted. The pack gives you the **official immigration sources**, plain-English context about what each page covers, and a tracker for the answers *you* get from officials or an agent you choose.
3. **"Are the rent numbers what I'll actually pay?"** — They're dated ranges from listings, clearly labelled estimates, not quotes. Use them to shortlist; confirm the actual price with the landlord or agent. We never present a single fixed rent.
4. **"Why pay when the matcher is free?"** — The matcher covers rail facts and the rent ranges that passed our publication gate. The pack adds the full 13-week plan, all six deep-dives, extended budget scenarios, the decisions tracker and quarterly refresh notes.
5. **"What if the information is wrong or moves on?"** — Every figure has a date; we refresh rents quarterly, transit semi-annually, and official links as they change; readers report corrections and we verify them. Flagged items are labelled "research candidate," not hidden.
6. **"I'm a family, is this relevant?"** — The core plan works; the pack adds family-oriented things to research early (school timelines, longer-stay housing patterns, healthcare/insurance official signposts). It won't pick schools or policies for you.
7. **"Is my payment and data safe?"** — Checkout is handled by an established payment provider that processes your card; we never see card details. We only ask for an email to deliver the pack, unsubscribe is one click, and we never ask for ID, visa, bank or medical documents.
8. **"What happens if you never finish it?"** — Every early-access reservation is refunded in full if the pack doesn't launch. Full refund terms: [Section 10 / reviewer-approved wording].
