# Part 1 — Bangkok First 7 Days Checklist (free)

Tone: calm, practical, sequential. Focus on **low-risk, practical** setup. Regulated items are signposts only.

## Before you fly (Day −7 → 0)
- [ ] Confirm your **visa/entry basis** and entry requirements for your nationality. **[REGULATED — VERIFY]** [C001][C007] → thaievisa.go.th, tdac.immigration.go.th.
- [ ] Submit the **digital arrival card (TDAC)** within the window the official portal specifies before each entry. **[REGULATED — VERIFY]** [C007]
- [ ] Book **temporary accommodation** for your first 7–14 nights near transit (serviced apartment / hotel), so you can flat-hunt without pressure. [REC]
- [ ] Unlock your phone; note your roaming status; plan an eSIM/SIM for arrival. [C010][C020]
- [ ] Tell your bank your travel dates; carry a card that works internationally and a small amount of local currency as backup.
- [ ] Save offline maps and the **arrival→city route** (Airport Rail Link / ride-hail / taxi). [C012][C013]
- [ ] Save **emergency numbers and the nearest 24-hour hospital** to your accommodation. [C014][C015]
- [ ] Buy **travel/health cover that starts on your arrival date**. *(Which plan is right for you is a personal/insurance decision — see the regulated notice.)* [REGULATED — VERIFY] [C006][C015]

## Day 1–2 — Arrival & getting around
- [ ] Clear immigration; keep the entry-stamp/visa documentation; note any reporting obligations your status carries. **[REGULATED — VERIFY]** [C001][C009]
- [ ] Get into the city: **Airport Rail Link** to connect to BTS/MRT, or a licensed taxi / ride-hail. Decide based on luggage and time of day. [C012][C013]
- [ ] Get a local **SIM or eSIM** (passport required for registration). Compare tourist eSIM vs in-store SIM for your stay length. [C010][C020]
- [ ] Install everyday apps: maps, ride-hail, messaging (Thailand primarily uses **LINE**), food/on-demand delivery, and your bank/card apps. [C013]
- [ ] Get a **transit stored-value card** for BTS/MRT to avoid single-ticket queues. [C011]
- [ ] Learn the BTS/MRT route from your temporary base to likely work/flat-hunt areas.

## Day 3–4 — Money, connectivity & work base
- [ ] Confirm how you'll **pay day-to-day**: contactless/cards are widespread in modern venues; cash still matters in markets/stalls; carry small notes.
- [ ] If you plan a **Thai bank account**, check your visa-specific eligibility **before** visiting branches. **[REGULATED — VERIFY]** [C004] → bank terms; bot.or.th. *(We do not advise on account/tax setups.)*
- [ ] Bring cross-border **money-transfer** options for when card/cash limits bite; compare fees. [C022]
- [ ] Settle a reliable **work base**: test your accommodation Wi‑Fi under load (video calls), and shortlist **coworking** spaces with backup internet. [C024]
- [ ] Set a daily work rhythm that accounts for **time zone** differences to your clients/employer.

## Day 5–7 — Housing & neighbourhood shortlist
- [ ] Treat the first two weeks as **research**, not commitment. Walk areas at different times of day (commute hour, night).
- [ ] Build a **shortlist of 3–5 neighbourhoods** using the framework in Part 2; score each on work, transit, convenience, noise and (if relevant) family fit.
- [ ] For each: check transit access, grocery/pharmacy proximity, walkability, and realistic **commute time** (map it at rush hour).
- [ ] Note **lease/rent realities** to verify later: deposit terms, minimum stay, utility billing, and whether the landlord handles address registration. **[REGULATED — VERIFY]** [C008]
- [ ] Avoid long-lease commitment until you've experienced the area in person. [REC]

## Health, safety & emergency (ongoing)
- [ ] Identify your nearest **accredited private hospital/clinic** and a 24-hour option; note direct-billing arrangements with your insurer. [C015]
- [ ] Save **emergency/medical and tourist-assistance numbers** in your phone and share them with someone back home. [C014]
- [ ] Keep digital copies of passport, visa/entry stamp, insurance policy and emergency contacts.
- [ ] Check **air quality** daily during pollution season and plan indoor days/purifiers if sensitive. [C018]
- [ ] Drinking water: use sealed/filtered water; basic food-and-water hygiene in the first weeks.
> 🏥 **Healthcare & insurance are personal decisions.** ThailandMate provides general pointers only. For medical treatment, prescriptions, or whether an insurance product meets a visa requirement, see a qualified provider and the regulator. [REGULATED — VERIFY] [C006][C015] → oic.or.th, longstay.tgia.org.

# Part 2 — Bangkok Neighbourhood Guide Framework (comparison model)

**Principle [REC]:** the guide is a **framework + verified data table**, not opinions. We publish no claim about a specific neighbourhood until it has a dated source. Naming an area is **not** an endorsement; all attributes start as `needs_verification`.

## 2.1 Scoring criteria (each scored 1–5 by the editorial team from verified inputs)
1. **Work suitability** — Wi‑Fi reliability, coworking/café density, quietness for calls, time-zone fit.
2. **Transport access** — BTS/MRT walk-in-reach, ride-hail availability, Airport Rail Link ease, traffic density.
3. **Family suitability** — space/value, green space, proximity to schools & family healthcare, safety feel, childcare. *(Families)*
4. **Everyday convenience** — grocery (incl. familiar/Western options), pharmacy, markets, malls, laundry, on-demand delivery coverage.
5. **Noise & pace** — daytime/night-time noise, nightlife density, street congestion, weekend vs weekday character.
6. **Housing research requirements** — stock type (condo/serviced/house), typical lease terms, deposit, utilities billing, supply/tightness — **all figures verified per listing; no price claims published without source**.
7. **Commute considerations** — door-to-door time at rush hour to (a) likely work/coworking hubs and (b) chosen school/workplace; transit vs car; walk-safety.

## 2.3 Housing research requirements (what the team must gather before publishing any area)
- Current **rent bands** by unit type, from dated listings + at least two sources; include deposit and typical lease length. **No prices in content until verified [REC].**
- Transit distances measured (walking minutes to nearest BTS/MRT) — not "close to."
- Rush-hour **commute timings** via mapping tool at 08:00 and 18:00.
- Proximity to: grocery, pharmacy, hospital/clinic, school (for families), coworking.
- Whether address registration (TM30) is supported by the landlord/building. **[REGULATED — VERIFY]** [C008]
- Noise/pace observed at multiple times; air-quality context for sensitive users. [C018]

## 2.4 Commute logic [REC]
- Remote workers optimise for **transit-access + coworking + airport ease**; families optimise for **home ↔ school** (Bangkok traffic means *live near the school*). [ASSUMPTION, widely supported in prior research; quantify per family with the tool.]
- Always present **door-to-door time at rush hour**, not distance.
