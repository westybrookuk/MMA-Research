# Brand Recommendation — Shift Shores / Land & Launch vs. the platform

**Date:** 29 August 2026. **Status:** recommendation based on the desk screen in `shiftshores-landandlaunch-due-diligence.md` (28-row check CSV). Not legal trademark clearance; all names remain `requires_professional_clearance` before use. Nothing registered.

---

## 1. Bottom line

- **Strongest global parent brand: LocalFooting** (the prior v2 finalist — destination-agnostic, inland-safe, all TLDs likely free, no in-sector conflict). See `global-brand-finalist-decision.md`.
- Neither founder proposal is recommended **as the global parent**:
  - **Shift Shores → reject as parent** (best use: newsletter/campaign, coastal only).
  - **Land & Launch → reject as parent** (best use: a product/programme name).
- **Strongest campaign / product name: "Land & Launch"** works well as the name of an **arrival sprint / the First 90 Days launch programme**; it should not name the company or place editions.

## 2. Why each candidate is rejected as the parent

### Shift Shores — reject for the parent role
1. **"Shores" means coast.** A platform serving Chiang Mai, Mexico City, Madrid, Berlin or São Paulo cannot carry a coastal word; the naming test fails on 3 of 5 cities (they have no shore). This is a global-architecture problem, not a stylistic one.
2. **Established meaning to overcome:** "Shifting Shores" is a known coastal-climate/erosion programme (National Trust) and academic project, and it carries a slow-erosion/climate-anxiety connotation; "shift/shores" is also crowded travel-media/podcast territory (Skift, SHIFT, ShiftShapers).
3. **Thai pronunciation is weak** (no natural "sh-or-es"; risks reading like "sure/ชัวร์"), and it pluralises into a thing ("shores") rather than a place.
- Keep, at most, as an **editorial/newsletter or coastal-campaign** name (it sounds like a magazine column) — after professional clearance.

### Land & Launch — reject for the parent role, keep as a product
1. **Concrete conflict:** `launchandland.com` is an **active web-design/development agency** ("Launch + Land", Minneapolis) — same concept in reverse order, in the web/SaaS field (class 42-adjacent) our tools occupy; reverse-order confusion is a real risk for an online product.
2. **Noisy phrase space:** "land and launch" is common in aerospace/rocket coverage and land-investing/flipping coaching (property/education, classes 36/41), so the mark is muddy even though the exact domain is free.
3. **Doesn't attach to places:** "Bangkok Land & Launch" reads as a programme being run *in* Bangkok, not Bangkok's edition — so it can't be the destination-edition pattern.
4. The word **"land"** also sits on the earlier excluded-root list (the platform moved away from Land/Nest/Base etc.), and Thai/global meaning leans to real-estate.
- Keep as a **product/programme name**: e.g. the arrival onboarding sprint within the First 90 Days pack ("Land & Launch: your first 30 days") — product names face lower collision risk and a class-42 clearance may still be needed.

## 4. Safe naming hierarchy

```
GLOBAL PARENT (company/platform/app):  LocalFooting
   tagline: "Get your footing in a new city."   (Thai: โลคัล ฟุตติ้ง)

DESTINATION EDITIONS  (place + root; works for coast AND inland):
   [Place] Footing
   Bangkok Footing   ·   Chiang Mai Footing   ·   Lisbon Footing
   Mexico City Footing   ·   Ho Chi Minh City Footing
   (paths: /bangkok  /chiang-mai  /lisbon  /mexico-city  /ho-chi-minh-city)

PRODUCTS (descriptive, under the parent):
   First 90 Days pack    →  "Footing First 90 Days: Bangkok"
   arrival sprint        →  "Land & Launch: your first 30 days"   (programme/product name; after clearance)
   free toolkit          →  "First 7 Days" checklist · Budget Planner · Neighbourhood Matcher
   newsletter (optional) →  "Shift Shores" as an editorial column name  (after clearance; coastal tone)

PROVIDER DIRECTORY:      "Footing Directory" (the verified-partner directory)
```

Rules:
- The **parent root** must be place-neutral (no coast/land words) so inland and future-city editions never contradict the name.
- Product/campaign names (Land & Launch, Shift Shores) can be evocative because they don't have to extend to places.
- Keep descriptive terms ("First 90 Days", "checklist", "matcher", "directory") un-branded; put trademark weight on the root.
- Destination edition format **`[Place] [Root]`** (not `[Brand]: [Place]`), which is the order that reads as the city's own edition.
